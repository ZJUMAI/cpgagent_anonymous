# TCGA-38-4625 Full Patient Report

## Pathology-Molecular Integration Summary

This case is a 66-year-old female with pathologic Stage IB (T2a N0 M0) lung adenocarcinoma of the right upper lobe. The tumor is a 5.0 cm, poorly differentiated, high-grade adenocarcinoma with marked pleomorphism, negative margins, and no lymph node involvement. Molecular profiling reveals a "common-driver-negative / CNV-dominant" pattern: no activating mutations in EGFR, KRAS, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, or NTRK1/2/3. The most significant molecular abnormality is deep deletion of CDKN2A (CNV = -2), a tumor suppressor gene associated with cell cycle dysregulation and poor prognosis. Copy gains are present in KRAS and NTRK1 (CNV = 1 each), but no high amplifications are observed. EGFR copy number is neutral, and no EGFR-TKI sensitivity inference is warranted. The molecular profile is consistent with a CNV-driven tumor lacking classic driver mutations, potentially relying on alternative mechanisms for tumor maintenance.

## Molecular Report

### Key Lung Cancer Molecular Interpretation

This case (TCGA-38-4625) is a 66-year-old female lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) patient. The molecular features are predominantly characterized by significant copy number variation (CNV), lacking activating mutations in classic driver genes, presenting a "common-driver-negative / CNV-dominant" pattern.

- **LUAD core driver/treatment-related genes:** No activating mutations were detected in EGFR, KRAS, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, NTRK1/2/3. Among these, KRAS and NTRK1 exhibit copy gain (CNV = 1), but no high amplification (CNV = 2). Notably, EGFR copy number is neutral, with no copy gain or high amplification; therefore, there is no issue regarding EGFR-TKI sensitivity inference related to EGFR copy gain.
- **LUAD tumor suppressor/prognosis/drug resistance-related genes:** No mutations were recorded in key tumor suppressor genes such as TP53, STK11, KEAP1, SMARCA4, RB1, NF1, and ATM. CDKN2A shows deep deletion (CNV = -2), a common tumor suppressor inactivation event in LUAD that may affect cell cycle regulation and is associated with poor prognosis.
- **Significant molecular abnormalities in this case:** CNV analysis reveals no high amplification genes (CNV = 2), but a large number of copy gains (1408 genes) and deep deletions (204 genes). The most prominent CNV events are CDKN2A deep deletion, and copy gains in KRAS and NTRK1. The RNA-seq Top 20 highly expressed genes (e.g., CRYGD, VGLL2, SERPINE2) do not directly support activation of key driver genes, but high SERPINE2 expression may be associated with tumor invasiveness. Overall, this case is predominantly CNV-driven, lacking typical driver mutations, suggesting possible reliance on other mechanisms (e.g., epigenetic alterations or non-coding RNA regulation) to maintain tumor growth.
