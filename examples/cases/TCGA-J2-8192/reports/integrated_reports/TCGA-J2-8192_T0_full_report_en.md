# TCGA-J2-8192 Full Patient Report

## Pathology-Molecular Integration Summary

This 65-year-old white female never-smoker presented with a 3.5 cm left upper lobe lung adenocarcinoma (AJCC stage IIA, pT2a pN1). The molecular profile identifies an EGFR exon 19 deletion with copy gain as the core driver event, consistent with the EGFR-driven LUAD subtype commonly seen in never-smokers. Concurrent deep deletions of CDKN2A, CDKN2B, and MTAP indicate impaired cell cycle regulation. KRAS, ALK, BRAF, ROS1, RET, and NTRK fusions are absent. The pathology report confirms EGFR exon 19 deletion and negative ALK FISH. The patient received pemetrexed/cisplatin starting 105 days post-diagnosis and erlotinib starting 641 days post-diagnosis, with a "Tumor Free" response at 154 days and alive at last follow-up (739 days). The EGFR copy gain, while present, does not independently predict EGFR-TKI sensitivity; the activating exon 19 deletion is the primary determinant.

## Molecular Report

### Key Lung Cancer Molecular Interpretation

This case involves a 65-year-old female with lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD). The molecular profile is characterized by **EGFR mutation with copy gain** as the core driver event, along with **deep deletion of CDKN2A/B and MTAP**, presenting a molecular landscape with a clear driver gene but concurrent inactivation of key tumor suppressor genes.

#### LUAD Core Driver/Therapeutic Genes
- **EGFR**: Mutation detected (exon 19 deletion) with **copy gain** (CNV=1, RNA-seq 4.0987). Note: EGFR copy gain is not equivalent to EGFR activating mutation and cannot independently infer EGFR-TKI sensitivity; however, combined with the presence of a mutation, this suggests the case may be EGFR-driven LUAD.
- **KRAS, ALK, BRAF, ROS1, RET, NTRK1/2/3**: No mutation or CNV abnormality recorded, excluding common alternative driver events.

#### LUAD Tumor Suppressor/Prognosis/Drug Resistance Genes
- **CDKN2A, CDKN2B, MTAP**: All show **deep deletion** (CNV=-2), with reduced CDKN2A RNA expression (-0.5801). CDKN2A deletion is a common tumor suppressor event in LUAD, associated with cell cycle dysregulation; MTAP deletion often co-occurs with CDKN2A deletion and may affect the methionine metabolism pathway.
- **TP53, STK11, KEAP1, SMARCA4, RB1, NF1, ATM**: No mutation or CNV abnormality recorded, suggesting relatively intact classic tumor suppressor pathways.

#### Significant Molecular Abnormalities in This Case
- **EGFR mutation + copy gain**: Clear driver event, with RNA-seq expression at a moderate level (4.0987), supporting its role as the primary oncogenic driver.
- **Deep deletion of CDKN2A/B and MTAP**: The only significant CNV deletion event, indicating impaired cell cycle regulation pathway.
- **No high amplification genes** (CNV=2), but 171 genes with copy gain, predominantly shallow deletions (428), indicating moderate overall genomic instability.

#### Molecular Pattern Summary
This case presents a molecular pattern of **EGFR-driven LUAD with CDKN2A/B-MTAP deletion**. EGFR mutation is a clear therapeutic target, but CDKN2A deletion may suggest a poor prognosis or potential sensitivity to cell cycle-targeted therapies such as CDK4/6 inhibitors. The RNA-seq Top 20 highly expressed genes (e.g., MACC1, SAV1) do not directly support key CNV events and are therefore not used as primary interpretation evidence.
