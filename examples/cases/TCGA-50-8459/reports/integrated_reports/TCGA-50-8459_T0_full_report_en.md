# TCGA-50-8459 Full Patient Report

## Pathology-Molecular Integration Summary

This case is a 68-year-old male with Stage IIB (pT3 N0 M0) mucinous adenocarcinoma of the right lower lobe. The pathology report confirms a KRAS exon 2 mutation (p.G12C, c.34G>T) and negative results for EGFR, BRAF, ALK, ROS1, and MET alterations. The molecular report identifies KRAS mutation with high amplification (CNV=2) as the core driver event, with no activating mutations or copy number alterations in other common driver genes (EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, NTRK). EGFR copy gain is absent (neutral CNV, no mutation), and therefore no association with EGFR-TKI sensitivity is implied. CDKN2A shows a shallow deletion (CNV=-1), a mild alteration of limited clinical significance. The tumor also demonstrates genomic instability with 25 high-amplification genes. The KRAS G12C mutation and high amplification together suggest potential applicability of KRAS-targeted therapy (e.g., KRAS G12C inhibitors), pending clinical correlation.

## Molecular Report

### Key Lung Cancer Molecular Interpretation

This case is lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD), with the molecular feature characterized by **KRAS mutation with high amplification** as the core driver event, while also presenting a **common-driver-negative / CNV-dominant** pattern, i.e., lacking activating mutations or copy number alterations in common driver genes such as EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, NTRK, etc.

- **Core driver/treatment-related genes**: KRAS has both a mutation and high amplification (CNV=2), suggesting this gene may drive tumorigenesis through a dual mechanism (mutational activation + copy number increase). EGFR shows neutral CNV and no mutation; therefore, **EGFR copy gain is absent**, and no association with TKI sensitivity needs to be considered. Other driver genes (ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, NTRK1/2/3) show no mutation or significant CNV alteration.
- **Tumor suppressor/prognosis/drug resistance-related genes**: Key tumor suppressor genes such as TP53, STK11, KEAP1, SMARCA4, RB1, NF1, and ATM show no mutation. CDKN2A shows a shallow deletion (CNV=-1), which may partially affect cell cycle regulation, but the deletion is mild and of limited clinical significance.
- **Significant molecular abnormalities in this case**: In addition to KRAS high amplification, 24 other genes (e.g., ARNTL2, BCAT1, BHLHE41, FGFR1OP2, ITPR2, etc.) show high amplification (CNV=2), suggesting genomic instability or active local amplification events. No deep deletion genes are present. The RNA-seq Top20 highly expressed genes (e.g., OR1A1, RTL1, MMP11, WNT5A, etc.) do not directly correspond to the above high-amplification genes, but MMP11, WNT5A, etc., are associated with tumor microenvironment remodeling and can serve as auxiliary biological background reference.

**Summary**: This LUAD case is primarily driven by KRAS mutation + high amplification, lacking other common driver mutations, and belongs to the CNV-dominant molecular subtype. The CDKN2A shallow deletion may be a secondary event. It is recommended to further evaluate the potential applicability of KRAS-targeted therapy (e.g., KRAS G12C inhibitors) in conjunction with clinical information.
