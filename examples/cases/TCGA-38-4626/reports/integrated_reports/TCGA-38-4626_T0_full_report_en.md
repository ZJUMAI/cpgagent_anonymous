# TCGA-38-4626 Full Patient Report

## Pathology-Molecular Integration Summary

This case is a 57-year-old female with poorly differentiated lung adenocarcinoma (5.3 cm, left upper lobe) and no evidence of lymph node metastasis, angiolymphatic invasion, or pleural involvement. The molecular profile reveals a **common-driver-negative / CNV-dominant** pattern: no activating mutations in EGFR, KRAS, BRAF, or other canonical LUAD drivers; an atypical ALK mutation without supporting CNV or RNA expression evidence; and widespread copy number gains (800 genes) with three high-amplification events (BCAT1, LRMP, RN7SL38P). The tumor shows 95% tumor cellularity and 75% tumor nuclei on pathology slides, consistent with a high-purity sample. No EGFR copy gain is present, and therefore EGFR-TKI sensitivity is not implied. The molecular features suggest possible reliance on copy number alteration-driven tumorigenesis rather than classic driver mutations.

## Molecular Report

### Key Lung Cancer Molecular Interpretation

This case (TCGA-38-4626) is a 57-year-old female lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) patient, with molecular features presenting a **common-driver-negative / CNV-dominant** pattern.

#### LUAD Core Driver/Treatment-Related Genes
- **EGFR, KRAS, BRAF**: No mutation recorded, CNV neutral, no significant RNA expression abnormality.
- **ALK**: Mutation present (atypical driver mutation), CNV data missing, no significant RNA expression elevation.
- **ROS1, RET, NTRK1/2/3**: No driver mutation or CNV abnormality recorded; ROS1 RNA expression is relatively high (1.92), but without CNV or mutation support, it does not constitute a clear driver event.
- **MET, ERBB2**: CNV data missing, no significant RNA expression abnormality.

#### LUAD Tumor Suppressor/Prognosis/Drug Resistance-Related Genes
- **TP53, STK11, KEAP1, SMARCA4, RB1, CDKN2A, NF1, ATM**: No mutation recorded, CNV all neutral or data missing, no significant RNA expression abnormality.
- No clear tumor suppressor gene inactivation event identified.

#### Significant Molecular Abnormalities in This Case
- **CNV Features**: Whole-genome CNV analysis shows 3 high amplification genes (BCAT1, LRMP, RN7SL38P), no deep deletion events. The number of high amplification genes is limited, but copy gain genes number up to 800, suggesting widespread chromosomal copy number increase.
- **RNA-seq Supporting Evidence**: Top 20 highly expressed genes are mainly immune/inflammation-related genes (e.g., CD300E, CD163, CCL13, C5AR1, etc.), with no clear driver gene or high amplification gene RNA overexpression support. High amplification genes (BCAT1, LRMP, RN7SL38P) do not appear in the RNA-seq Top 20; their functional significance requires evaluation with other evidence.
- **No EGFR copy gain**: EGFR CNV is neutral, no need to discuss EGFR-TKI sensitivity.

#### Summary
This case lacks common LUAD driver mutations (EGFR, KRAS, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, NTRK, etc.), but exhibits widespread copy gains and a small number of high amplification events, consistent with the **common-driver-negative / CNV-dominant** molecular subtype. ALK has an atypical mutation, but without CNV or RNA expression support for its driver role. The overall molecular features suggest possible reliance on non-canonical driver mechanisms or copy number alteration-driven tumorigenesis; further integration of clinical and pathological information is needed to evaluate therapeutic targets.
