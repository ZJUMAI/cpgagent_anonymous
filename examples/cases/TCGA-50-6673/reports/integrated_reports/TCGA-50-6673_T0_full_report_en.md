# TCGA-50-6673 Full Patient Report

## Pathology-Molecular Integration Summary

This case is a stage I (T1 N0 M0) lung adenocarcinoma in an 84-year-old female lifelong non-smoker. The pathology report identified EGFR amplification (FISH ratio 9.33) and low-level HER2/neu amplification (ratio 1.40). The molecular report confirms EGFR mutation with high amplification (CNV=2) as the core driver event, accompanied by TP53 mutation and CDKN2A shallow deletion. The EGFR copy gain is not equivalent to an EGFR activating mutation and does not by itself imply EGFR-TKI sensitivity; however, the presence of a definite EGFR mutation in this case, combined with high amplification, may further enhance pathway activity. No other common driver gene abnormalities (KRAS, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, NTRK family) were identified. The patient received internal radiation therapy (1 mCi over 50 fractions) with progressive disease and died 22 days after diagnosis.

## Molecular Report

### Key Lung Cancer Molecular Interpretation

This case (TCGA-50-6673) is lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD), with molecular features characterized by **EGFR mutation with high amplification** as the core driver event, accompanied by **TP53 mutation** and **CDKN2A shallow deletion**.

- **Core Driver/Treatment-Related Genes**: **EGFR** harbors both mutation and high amplification (CNV=2), with a relatively high RNA expression level (11.08). Note that EGFR copy gain is not equivalent to EGFR activating mutation and cannot independently infer EGFR-TKI sensitivity; however, combined with the definite EGFR mutation in this case, high amplification may further enhance pathway activity. No definite driver mutations or significant CNV abnormalities were observed in other common driver genes (KRAS, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, ERBB2, RET, NTRK family).
- **Tumor Suppressor/Prognosis/Drug Resistance-Related Genes**: **TP53** harbors a mutation, which is the most common co-occurring mutation in LUAD, typically associated with genomic instability and poorer prognosis. **CDKN2A** shows shallow deletion (CNV=-1), which may partially affect cell cycle regulation. No definite abnormalities were found in key tumor suppressor genes such as STK11, KEAP1, SMARCA4, RB1, NF1, ATM.
- **Significant Molecular Abnormalities in This Case**: In addition to EGFR, high amplification genes include **DEPTOR, LANCL2, SEC61G, VOPP1, VSTM2A**. Among these, LANCL2 also appears in the RNA-seq Top20 high expression list (17.65), suggesting enhanced transcriptional activity. No deep deletion genes were identified. The overall CNV profile is dominated by copy gains (468 genes) and shallow deletions (239 genes), with limited high amplification events.
- **Pattern Summary**: This case presents a classic LUAD molecular subtype characterized by **EGFR mutation with high amplification as the driver, co-occurring with TP53 mutation**, with no abnormalities in other common driver genes.
